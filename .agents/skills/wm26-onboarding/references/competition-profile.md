# WM26 Competition Profile

Use this profile only for `fifa-world-cup-2026`. The runtime source is `CompetitionCollectionProfileResolver`; [ADR-0034](../../../../plans/bundesliga-2026-27/decisions/0034-drive-context-collection-from-competition-profiles.md) records the preserved WM26 branch. Before an operation, read [WM26 operational safety](operational-safety.md), [the model ledger](../../../../docs/onboarding-wm26/model-config-onboarding.md), and the relevant section of [the WM26 onboarding record](../../../../docs/onboarding-wm26/README.md).

## Contract

- **Identity and teams:** Development community `ehonda-dev-wm26`; reference production `rabetrabauken2026`; 48 teams; 104 matches; variable matchday size; season `2026-06-11` through `2026-07-19`. Team membership comes from the official FIFA full-squad seed in `data/wm26/lineups/` and `data/wm26/lineups/wm26-teams.csv`, with `dcaribou/transfermarkt-datasets` DuckDB as the permitted supplement only.
- **Collectors:** Run `Kicktipp` → `Wm26HistoryPlayedDates` → `FifaRankings` → `NationalLineups` through `collect-context profile`/`collect-context-dev`; the latter three are direct phases. Do not invoke `BundesligaHistoryPlayedDates`, `ClubElo`, or `Rosters`.
- **Required context:** `fifa-world-cup-2026-standings.csv`, community rules and knockout rules, per-team recent history/FIFA ranking/lineup documents, and KPI documents `fifa-rankings` and `lineups`. WM26 uses knockout rules, not home/away or head-to-head history. Use `data/wm26/recent-history/recent-history-match-dates.csv` and preserve its `Played_At` contract.
- **Prompts/models:** Hosted routes are `kicktippai/wm26/predict-one-match` and `kicktippai/wm26/predict-bonus`, label `latest`, with checked-in `wm26` fallback. Development is only `gpt-5-nano` / `minimal` / cap `10000`; selected production is `o3` / `high` / cap `40000`. All routes use the runtime `flex-first-standard-fallback` policy defined in operational safety; the ledger owns each active model's explicit workflow wiring and observed tier evidence.
- **Costs and communities:** Use the 104-match whole-competition assumption, the exact estimator command, and missing-row procedure in operational safety. The ledger owns posting target, community context, credential pair, telemetry environment, and copy/self-contained semantics. A posting identity must manually join the target; credentials always follow the posting target, not a reused context.
- **Validation and activation:** Use the no-write full-profile preflight and strict date-map dry-run in operational safety, then the deterministic collection/date-map tests. A separately authorized dev write needs context→matchday→bonus plus final verifier and Langfuse checks. The model ledger and onboarding record own active workflow state, first-run evidence, owner authority, and rollback; do not infer activation from the historical WM26 schedule state.

WM26-specific source, format, workflow, secrets, cost, first-run, snapshot, and rollback requirements are required reads in [WM26 operational safety](operational-safety.md). This profile never calls Bundesliga collectors, data paths, prompts, community matrix, or activation lane.
